About Savannah Tree Removal Co

A locally-focused tree service built around one thing: connecting Savannah homeowners with an experienced, licensed crew, fast.

Who We Are

Locally owned, licensed, and experienced

Savannah Tree Removal Co connects homeowners across Savannah and Chatham County with a licensed local tree crew that's been doing this work for over 10 years. We're not a national call-center dispatch reading from a script – every call gets a straight answer, and every job starts with a free, no-obligation estimate before anything happens.

We handle tree removal, emergency and storm cleanup, large and hazardous takedowns, trimming and pruning, and stump grinding across Savannah's neighborhoods – from newer subdivisions in Pooler to century-old live oaks in the Historic District. Different areas come with different considerations (HOA approval at The Landings, city tree permits downtown, marsh access on Wilmington Island), and we plan around them instead of treating every yard the same.

Built for coastal Georgia

Hurricane season (June–November), salt air, and Savannah's old live oak canopy all mean local tree work looks different here than it does inland. We work with those conditions, not against them.

How We Work

What you can expect

  • A free, on-site estimate before any work starts – no phone-guess pricing, no surprise invoice.
  • Real equipment sized to the job – chainsaws, chippers, and rigging, not a single guy with a handsaw.
  • Full cleanup and haul-away every time – logs, brush, and sawdust gone before we leave.
  • Straight answers about permits, HOA rules, or access issues specific to your neighborhood – not left for you to figure out.
