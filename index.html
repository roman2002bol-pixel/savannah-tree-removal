Built for the Lowcountry

Tree work suited to coastal Georgia

Savannah's old oak canopy and Spanish moss are part of what makes the city beautiful – and part of why a leaning or storm-weakened live oak needs a crew that knows how these trees actually behave, not a generic landscaping outfit. Hurricane season (June–November) and sudden summer storms bring down limbs fast, and salt air and heavy rain speed up root and trunk decay in ways an inland crew won't always catch early.

FAQ

Tree removal in Savannah – common questions

How much does tree removal cost in Savannah?

Most jobs run a few hundred to a few thousand dollars depending on the tree's height, trunk diameter, how accessible it is for equipment, and how close it is to a structure or power line. We give you a firm number after a free on-site look – not a guess over the phone.

Do you handle storm and emergency tree removal?

Yes – if a tree is down on your home, car, or driveway, that's exactly the kind of job we prioritize. Call the number above and describe the situation.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Savannah?

The City of Savannah regulates removal of certain protected trees, particularly in the Historic District and for larger specimen trees. We'll flag it if your job needs a permit and can talk you through what that involves.

What areas do you serve?

Savannah and the surrounding Chatham County area, including Pooler, Richmond Hill, Wilmington Island, Skidaway Island, Georgetown, Isle of Hope, and the Historic District. Not sure if you're covered? Just ask.

Have a tree that needs to come down?

Free estimate, no pressure – just a straight answer on price and timing.
