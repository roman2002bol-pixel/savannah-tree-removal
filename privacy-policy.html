Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

What we collect

When you call, text, email, or submit the estimate or contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly – typically your name, phone number, email address, property address or neighborhood, and details about the tree work you need. We don't collect anything beyond what you choose to give us.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your request: to follow up, schedule a free estimate, and provide the tree service you asked about. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Form submissions

This site's forms currently send your submission to us by email. We don't run any tracking or advertising pixels beyond standard, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use the site.

Contacting us about your information

If you'd like us to delete information you've previously sent us, email info@savannahtreeremovalco.com and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page with a new "last updated" date above.
