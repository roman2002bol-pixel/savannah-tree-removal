Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing, service area, licensing, permits, and HOA rules – the questions we hear most, answered straight.

About us & how we work

Pricing & Process

What to expect

How much does tree removal cost in Savannah?

Most jobs run a few hundred to a few thousand dollars, depending on the tree's height and trunk diameter, how accessible it is for our equipment, and how close it is to a structure, fence, or power line. We confirm a firm price after a free on-site look, not a phone guess.

Do you remove the stump too, or just the tree?

Standard tree removal leaves the stump behind. Stump grinding is a separate service we can quote at the same visit.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency tree removal?

Storm damage doesn't wait for regular hours, so we prioritize emergency calls for trees down on a home, car, driveway, or blocking a road. Call us and we'll give you an honest answer on how quickly we can get there.

What happens to the wood and debris?

Full cleanup and haul-away is included on every job – we don't leave logs, brush, or sawdust behind. If you want to keep firewood, just let us know before we start.

Local Rules

Permits, HOAs, and neighborhood-specific quirks

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Savannah's Historic District?

In most cases, yes. Savannah's Tree Protection Ordinance protects live oaks citywide, so removing one on private property generally requires a Tree Removal Permit backed by a certified arborist's assessment, and an approved removal usually requires a replacement tree. We handle this process for you – see the Historic District page for details.

Do you handle tree removal at HOA communities like The Landings?

Yes. At The Landings on Skidaway Island, for example, any tree 20 inches or more in circumference on a developed property needs the Association's Public Works approval first – we help you navigate that process instead of it holding up the work.
