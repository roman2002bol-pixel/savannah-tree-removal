FAQ

Stump grinding – common questions

Does stump grinding remove the whole root system?

No. Grinding removes the visible stump and the top portion of the root flare below grade, so you can plant grass, mulch, or a garden bed over it. The deeper root system stays in the ground and breaks down naturally over the following years.

Can I replant in the same spot after grinding?

Grass and shallow-rooted plants, yes, right away. For a new tree in the exact same spot, we usually recommend waiting or shifting a couple feet over so it isn't competing with the old root system as it breaks down.
