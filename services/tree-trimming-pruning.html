FAQ

Tree trimming & pruning – common questions

How often should trees be trimmed?

Most established trees benefit from trimming every 2-3 years; young trees and ones near your roof or power lines often need a look every year. A free estimate visit can tell you what your specific trees need.

Will trimming near power lines hurt the tree?

Not when it's done correctly. Limbs growing into lines are one of the most common reasons Savannah homeowners call us – it's both a safety issue and often a utility requirement. We prune to protect the tree's shape and long-term health, not just cut a straight line along the wire.

How much does tree trimming cost in Savannah?

It depends on the number of trees, their height, and how much needs to come off. Trimming is typically less expensive than a full removal. We confirm a firm price after a free on-site look.
