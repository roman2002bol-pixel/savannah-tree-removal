Pricing

How much does tree removal cost in Savannah?

Most jobs run a few hundred to a few thousand dollars. The final number depends on the tree's height and trunk diameter, how close it is to your house or power lines, and how much room our equipment has to work. We confirm the exact price on-site, before any work begins – never a surprise invoice.

FAQ

Tree removal – common questions

How much does tree removal cost in Savannah?

Most jobs run a few hundred to a few thousand dollars depending on the tree's height, trunk diameter, how accessible it is for our equipment, and how close it is to a structure, fence, or power line. We confirm a firm price after a free on-site look, not a phone guess.

Do you remove the stump too, or just the tree?

Standard tree removal leaves the stump behind. If you want it gone, stump grinding is a separate service we can quote at the same visit.

What happens to the wood and debris?

Full cleanup and haul-away is included – we don't leave logs, brush, or sawdust behind. If you want to keep firewood, just let us know before we start.

Do you serve my neighborhood?

We cover Savannah and the surrounding Chatham County area, including Pooler, Richmond Hill, Wilmington Island, Skidaway Island, Georgetown, Isle of Hope, and the Historic District. If you're just outside these, reach out and we'll confirm.
